Our Story

Founded on a love of beauty, built for confidence.

Maybella Beauty was founded in 2023 by May Balabbo, born from a personal passion for skincare that grew into a full beauty brand. The name blends "May" and "Bella": beauty, confidence and elegance in every product.

Our mission is simple: premium beauty products that inspire confidence and deliver real value, without the premium price tag.
