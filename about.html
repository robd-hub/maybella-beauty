Our Story

How Maybella began

Maybella Beauty is a proudly Philippine beauty and skincare brand founded by May Balabbo. Founded in 2023, it was created with a vision of providing high-quality, innovative and affordable beauty products that empower confidence and celebrate natural beauty.

What started as a passion for skincare has grown into a trusted beauty brand offering cosmetics, skincare, lip products and other beauty essentials for customers in the Philippines and around the world.

The name Maybella combines "May" and "Bella", representing beauty, confidence and elegance. Every product is thoughtfully developed with quality, safety and customer satisfaction in mind.

Our Mission

To create premium beauty products that inspire confidence, enhance natural beauty, and deliver exceptional value through innovation, quality and care.

Our Vision

To become one of the most trusted Philippine beauty brands, recognized globally for excellence, innovation and customer satisfaction.

Today, Maybella Beauty continues to grow through innovation, authenticity and a commitment to empowering women while delivering products customers can trust. Thank you for being part of the Maybella Beauty journey.
